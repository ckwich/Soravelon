## Critical Bug Fixed During Audit

**value_scales field mismatch** (commit 1e849c9): `item_spawner.py` wrote `db.value` but `vendor_engine`, `cmd_inspect`, and `inventory_engine` read `db.value_scales`. All spawner-created items (loot, crafting, vendor) had value_scales=0, breaking sell/appraise/inspect. Fixed by setting both fields.
