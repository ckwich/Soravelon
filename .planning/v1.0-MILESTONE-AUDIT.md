## Critical Gaps (Blockers)

### 1. Mob Ability Resolution (CRITICAL)
**Affects:** CMB-01, CMB-03
**Problem:** `combat_script._process_mob_turn()` calls `use_ability(mob, ability_id)` with mob-template ability IDs (e.g., "wolf_bite") that don't exist in `ability_registry.ABILITIES`. The registry only has 330 player abilities. Every mob special ability silently fails.
**Fix:** Bridge mob ability resolution — combat_script should resolve mob abilities through their inline data (damage_base, status_effect, element) rather than routing through the player ability pipeline.

### 2. Equipment Non-Functional (HIGH)
**Affects:** CON-04
**Problem:** 76 equipment items exist with armor_value, stat_bonuses, damage_min/max, but:
- No CmdEquip/CmdUnequip commands exist
- combat_engine never reads armor_value from equipped items
- base_attributes never reads stat_bonuses from equipped items
**Fix:** Create equip/unequip commands, wire armor into damage formula, wire stat_bonuses into stat calculation.

### 3. Trainer NPCs Unwired (HIGH)
**Affects:** SKL-01, SKL-03
**Problem:** TRAINER_REGISTRY has 21 entries but no zone NPC has trainer_id binding. CmdTrain always fails.
**Fix:** Wire trainer_id to zone NPCs (tag or db attribute).

### 4. Zone Scaling Not Applied (MEDIUM)
**Affects:** CMB-02
**Problem:** zone_scaling module exists and is tested but never called from combat_engine during actual combat.
**Fix:** Wire zone_scaling.calculate_player_scale_factor() into damage resolution.

### 5. Domain Resource on Guild Join (MEDIUM)
**Affects:** ABL-02, DOM-03
**Problem:** initialize_domain_resource only called at login. Mid-session guild join leaves player without domain resource until relog.
**Fix:** Call initialize_domain_resource after join_guild succeeds.
