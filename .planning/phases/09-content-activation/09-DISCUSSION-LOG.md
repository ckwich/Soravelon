## Wilderness NPC Density

**User's choice:** No fixed NPC-per-zone quota. Organic placement driven by zone flavor:
- Merchants only where traffic/loot exists naturally
- Trainers thematically matched to terrain
- Faction agents sparse and selective
- Rest camps occasional, not guaranteed

**Critical constraint:** NEVER remove existing NPCs from any zone without express user authorization.
