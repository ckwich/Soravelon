# Varath Prime Hub 4 Continuity Packet

**Date:** 2026-04-20
**Purpose:** Shared naming, decree, and institutional packet for `varath_prime` and the four connected Hub 4 exterior zones.

## Use Rules
- Reuse these names exactly across all five Hub 4 zones.
- If a new area references one of these institutions, seals, or houses, use the same spelling and phrasing.
- If a plaque, ledger, or oral account contradicts another source, the contradiction should still reuse the same decree, year, house, or office name.
- Nothing here should imply dragons are secretly lucid beneath the curse.

## Core Institutions

### Human Empire offices
- **High Registry of Roads and Passage**
  - Handles manifests, relay traffic, toll records, and roadway decrees.
- **Office of Seals and Civic Measure**
  - Handles permits, charter copying, public inscriptions, civic surveys, and official revisions.

### Dragon Corps
- **Thirteenth Talon Command**
  - Regional Dragon Corps command around Varath Prime.
  - Shows up on rosters, courier tags, tack stamps, and flight manifests.

### Circle presence
- **Surveyor Collegium of Varath Prime**
  - Circle-sanctioned survey and containment office used for road geometry, ruin review, and sanctioned anomaly notation.

### Contractor / extraction interest
- **Stonewake Contracting**
  - Appears on quarry requisitions, lift parts, haul stamps, and labor invoices.

### Hidden resistance line
- **The Cinder Ledger**
  - Resistance logistics thread using pamphlets, copied manifests, and altered registry leaves.

## Noble Houses
- **House Talvere**
  - Closely tied to roads, public order, and ceremonial capital works.
- **House Rhest**
  - Closely tied to charters, preserve privileges, and private patronage around the capital.

## Shared Decrees and Public Text

### 706 - Edict of Measured Passage
- Used on milestones, traffic boards, relay ledgers, and checkpoint language.
- Public framing: order, safety, lawful transit.
- Private implication: movement surveillance and disappearance by paperwork.

### 711 - Crown Restoration Charter
- Used on processional paving, culvert repairs, and reused imperial plaques.
- Public framing: restoration of neglected crown roads.
- Private implication: renaming and repurposing older works.

### 728 - Stagcrown Revision
- Used on preserve boundary stones, noble lodge records, and ranger logs.
- Public framing: habitat preservation and lawful hunting.
- Private implication: enclosure, privilege, and controlled access.

### 734 - Iron Vein Requisition
- Used on quarry records, haul manifests, memorial stones, and ministry requisition copies.
- Public framing: emergency civic improvement and defensive necessity.
- Private implication: intensified extraction and labor brutality.

## Shared Seal and Mark Vocabulary
- **talon-and-bar seal**
  - Use for Thirteenth Talon Command rosters and courier manifests.
- **measured rod seal**
  - Use for Office of Seals and Civic Measure copies, decree boards, and survey postings.
- **split roadwheel stamp**
  - Use for High Registry of Roads and Passage toll marks and relay manifests.
- **stonewake pickmark**
  - Use for Stonewake Contracting crates, tool heads, and quarry invoices.
- **ash-cinder cipher**
  - Use for Cinder Ledger pamphlets, print proofs, and altered registry leaves.

## Shared Phrasing
- Public inscription style:
  - "restored to proper measure"
  - "returned to lawful passage"
  - "set under revised charter"
  - "recorded under civic seal"
- Private ledger style:
  - "prior text removed"
  - "copy accepted in place of stone"
  - "disposition omitted by instruction"
  - "entry reassigned without witness"
